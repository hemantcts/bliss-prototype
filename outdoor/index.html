Frequently Asked Questions

Outdoor FAQ

Should I choose natural gas or propane?

Natural gas is convenient if you have a supply line nearby; propane offers flexibility in placement. Many grills can be converted with the right kit.

Can outdoor grills stay outside in winter?

Stainless steel grills can stay outside with a fitted cover. Store propane tanks upright outdoors, away from the house.

Do you ship outdoor products across Canada and the USA?

Yes. Large items ship by freight with a scheduled delivery appointment.
