Frequently Asked Questions

Kitchen FAQ

How do I choose a kitchen faucet that fits my sink?

Match the faucet reach to the middle of the sink basin and make sure the spout height clears your tallest pots. Check the number of holes in your sink or countertop too.

What is a workstation sink?

A workstation sink has a built-in ledge for accessories such as cutting boards, colanders and drying racks that slide along the sink, turning it into a prep station.
