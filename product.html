Frequently Asked Questions

What size is the Victoria and Albert Barcelona 2 bathtub?

67" long × 31" wide × 24" high (1700 × 790 × 610 mm), holding approximately 80 gallons.

What materials are used in the construction?

QUARRYCAST®, a blend of crushed volcanic limestone and high-performance resin, finished by hand.

Can I get the Barcelona 2 in a different colour or finish?

Yes. Choose gloss or matte white, or a custom exterior colour. Custom finishes add 4–6 weeks.

Does the bathtub come with a drain and overflow?

Yes, a matching click-clack waste with integrated overflow is included. Finish-matched options are available.

Is professional installation required?

We strongly recommend a licensed plumber. Our team can refer trusted installers in your area.

How do I clean and maintain the cast stone finish?

Use warm water and mild soap with a soft cloth. Avoid abrasive pads and bleach-based cleaners.

What is the lead time for delivery?

In-stock finishes typically ship in 2–5 business days, then arrive in about 2–5 business days. Custom colours are made to order; we'll email an estimated lead time.

Do you offer a warranty on Victoria and Albert products?

Yes, a 25-year limited manufacturer's warranty on the tub body, and 2 years on the waste.
