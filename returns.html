    Full Return Policy

    Returns, Cancellations & Damaged Items

    If your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, our team will work with you to resolve the issue as quickly as possible. Because many of the products we sell are fragile, oversized or specially ordered from manufacturers, it is important that all products are inspected immediately upon delivery and before installation.

    For assistance, contact us at admin@blissbathandkitchen.com or 1-855-366-1001.

    Damaged & Defective Products

    Although we take care to ensure products are properly shipped, merchandise may occasionally be damaged during transportation or arrive with a manufacturer defect. If this happens, Bliss Bath & Kitchen will work with you, the manufacturer and/or the shipping carrier to help resolve the issue.

    Customers are responsible for inspecting merchandise immediately upon delivery and reporting any damage within the required timeframe.

    Please do not install a product that appears damaged, defective or incorrect.

    Small-parcel deliveries

    If a small-parcel shipment arrives damaged, please do not automatically refuse the package. Accept the delivery and immediately photograph:

    • The outside of the shipping box
    • The shipping label
    • Any visible damage to the packaging
    • The damaged product
    • The product model or identifying information, where applicable

    Contact our Customer Service team immediately at 1-855-366-1001. Claims reported within 7 days of delivery may be submitted to the carrier; however, please notify us within one business day whenever possible to avoid delays. Failure to report damage promptly may affect our ability to obtain a replacement or refund from the carrier or manufacturer.

    Large & freight deliveries

    Immediate inspection is especially important for large products such as bathtubs, vanities and large sinks delivered by freight carrier. Please inspect the shipment before signing the carrier's delivery receipt or Bill of Lading.

    If there is any visible damage to the packaging or merchandise, even if it appears minor, clearly write “DAMAGED” and describe the damage on the Bill of Lading before signing. We strongly recommend photographing the packaging and merchandise while the carrier is still present.

    All freight damage must be reported within 24 hours of delivery. If the product appears significantly damaged, you may refuse the delivery. Please contact us immediately if a shipment is refused. Customers who sign for a freight delivery without noting visible damage may assume responsibility for damage that could have been documented at delivery.

    Damaged or defective product resolution

    When merchandise is confirmed to have arrived damaged or defective, we will arrange an appropriate resolution, which may include repair, replacement parts or replacement of the product at no additional product cost. The exact resolution may depend on the nature of the issue and the applicable manufacturer's warranty or carrier claim procedure.

    If a customer chooses to return an item rather than accept an available repair or replacement, the return may be treated as a standard customer return and applicable shipping and restocking charges may apply. If a returned product is inspected and found to be in proper working condition and shipped as ordered, the return may be treated as a standard customer return, subject to the 25% restocking fee and shipping charges.

    Cancellations

    Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel an order. Orders may be cancelled prior to shipment, delivery or pickup when possible. Any costs already incurred at the time of cancellation may be deducted from the refund.

    Special-order, custom and made-to-order products cannot be cancelled or returned once the order has been placed with the manufacturer. Orders cancelled after shipping, or after being made available for delivery or pickup, will be treated as returns. Approved cancellation refunds are issued to the original purchaser and original method of payment.

    Returns & Return Goods Authorization

    All returns require prior authorization and are subject to a 25% restocking fee plus shipping. Before returning any merchandise, email admin@blissbathandkitchen.com to request a Return Goods Authorization (RGA). Include:

    • Your name
    • Order number
    • Product being returned
    • Reason for the return
    • Photos of the product and packaging, if requested

    Please wait until you have received written approval and an RGA number before shipping. Returns sent without an RGA number may be refused, and any associated charges will be the responsibility of the sender.

    Return period & condition

    Requests to return non-damaged products must be submitted within 14 days of receiving the merchandise. To qualify, merchandise must be new, unused and uninstalled; in new or like-new condition; in its original, undamaged manufacturer's packaging and crating; and complete with all original parts, accessories, manuals and instructions. All returns are inspected before a refund or credit is issued.

    Non-returnable items

    • Products that have been partially or completely installed
    • Products that have been assembled
    • Custom, made-to-order or special-order products
    • Discontinued products, unless confirmed to be defective
    • Floor models
    • Products identified as “Off Price”, clearance or similar
    • Products sold in a condition indicating they are not first-quality merchandise
    • Products without their original packaging or that cannot be resold as new

    Certain manufacturers may have additional return restrictions, which will also apply.

    Restocking fees

    Approved customer-requested returns are subject to a 25% restocking/re-handling fee. Original and return shipping charges are non-refundable and will be deducted where applicable. If the return is due to our error, confirmed shipping damage or a manufacturer issue, Bliss Bath & Kitchen will be responsible for the applicable return shipping costs.

    Return shipping

    Unless the return is due to our error, shipping damage or another issue not caused by the purchaser, the customer is responsible for arranging the return carrier, return shipping charges, properly packaging the merchandise and insuring the shipment. Approved returns must be shipped using insured ground transportation unless we instruct otherwise. Where we are responsible, we will provide instructions and arrange and pay for return freight and insurance.

    Refused deliveries

    Please do not refuse a delivery unless there is significant visible shipping damage or you have been instructed to do so. A freight delivery refused for any other reason will be treated as a customer-requested return; return freight, original shipping, additional carrier charges and the 25% restocking fee may be deducted from the refund.

    Refunds

    Returned merchandise must be received and fully inspected before a refund or replacement is processed. The refund is based on the original product purchase price, less applicable restocking fees, original shipping charges, return shipping charges and other carrier charges. Refunds are issued to the original purchaser using the original method of payment; credit card purchases are refunded to the original card. Please allow additional time for your bank to post the refund.

    Need help before ordering? Not sure about a finish, size, configuration or compatibility? Our product specialists can help you compare options and select products that work together. Contact a specialist.

    Return eligibility and manufacturer policies may vary by product. Nothing in this policy limits any rights or remedies available to customers under applicable consumer protection laws.
