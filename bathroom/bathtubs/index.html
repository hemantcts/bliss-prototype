Buying guide

Bathtubs for Every Bathroom and Budget

A bathtub sets the tone for the whole bathroom. Choose a sculptural freestanding tub as a centrepiece, a clawfoot for classic character, or a corner or non-standard tub to make the most of a smaller or unusual space.

Compare materials before you decide: acrylic is light and warm to the touch, stone resin offers a solid matte finish and excellent heat retention, and cast iron is the most durable of all. Check the overall length, interior depth and drain position against your plans.

Size and clearance

Allow at least 6 inches around a freestanding tub for cleaning and plumbing access.

Material

Acrylic, stone resin and cast iron each feel and wear differently.

Matching tub filler

Pair a freestanding tub with a floor-mounted tub filler in the same finish as your faucets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Bathtubs FAQ

What type of bathtub is best for a small bathroom?

Compact freestanding tubs from 54 inches, corner tubs and Japanese soaking tubs make the most of limited floor space while still offering a deep, comfortable soak.

How long does bathtub delivery take?

In-stock bathtubs typically ship within 2–5 business days and travel by freight with a scheduled delivery appointment. Made-to-order and special-finish tubs can take 6–8 weeks.

What is the difference between acrylic, stone resin and cast iron tubs?

Acrylic is lightweight and affordable, stone resin is solid with a velvety matte finish and better heat retention, and cast iron is extremely durable and holds heat longest but is very heavy.

Do bathtubs come with a drain and overflow?

Most freestanding tubs include a drain, and some include an integrated overflow. Each product lists exactly what is included, or ask us to confirm before you order.

Can I return a bathtub?

Eligible unused items in original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Inspect your tub on delivery and note any visible damage on the delivery receipt.
