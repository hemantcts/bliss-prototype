Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about freestanding bathtubs.

What size freestanding tub do I need?

Allow at least 6" of clearance around the tub for cleaning and plumbing access. Tubs from 54" to 60" suit most bathrooms, while 66"+ models suit larger primary suites.

Are freestanding tubs difficult to install?

Installation is straightforward for a licensed plumber. You'll need a floor drain in the correct position and either a floor-mounted or wall-mounted tub filler.

What materials are best for a freestanding bathtub?

Acrylic is light and warm to the touch; stone resin offers a solid, matte finish and excellent heat retention; cast iron is the most durable and traditional choice.

How do I clean and maintain a freestanding tub?

Use a soft cloth with mild, non-abrasive cleaner. Avoid bleach and scouring pads, and rinse after each use to keep the finish looking new.

Can I get a freestanding tub in a different color?

Many models are available in matte black, two-tone and custom RAL colours. Look for the colour swatches on each product, or ask our team about special orders.

Do you have a showroom I can visit?

What is the average price range?

Our freestanding tubs range from about $1,800 for quality acrylic models to $12,000+ for handcrafted stone and cast iron designs.

Are freestanding tubs good for small bathrooms?

Yes. Compact models from 54" and slim-profile designs make a strong visual statement without overwhelming a smaller room.
