Frequently Asked Questions

Bathroom FAQ

What should I choose first when designing a bathroom?

Start with the largest or most visible piece, usually the bathtub or vanity, then choose faucets and fixtures in a finish that complements it. Measuring the space and confirming plumbing locations early avoids surprises later.

Can I match finishes across different brands?

Yes, but shades vary between manufacturers: brushed gold from one brand may be warmer than another. Ask us for finish samples, or keep one brand for all visible metal fixtures.

Do you ship bathroom products across Canada and the USA?

Yes. Eligible orders ship free across Canada and the USA. Large items such as bathtubs and vanities ship by freight, and we confirm a delivery appointment with you.

Do you offer installation?

We don’t install directly, but we can recommend licensed plumbers and contractors in the Greater Toronto Area and share specification sheets with any installer.

Can I see your bathroom products in person?

Yes. Visit our showroom at 5 Shields Court, Unit 104, Markham, Ontario. Book an appointment for dedicated time with a specialist.
