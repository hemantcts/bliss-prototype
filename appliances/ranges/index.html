Ranges FAQ

How do I choose the right ranges?

Start with the size and installation requirements of your space, then choose a style and finish that coordinates with the rest of the room. Our specialists are happy to review your plans and recommend options.

Do you ship ranges across Canada and the USA?

Yes. Eligible orders ship free across Canada and the USA. Large items ship by freight with a scheduled delivery appointment.

Can I see ranges in person?

Many products are on display at our showroom at 5 Shields Court, Unit 104, Markham, Ontario. Book an appointment for dedicated time with a specialist.

Do you offer trade or project pricing?

Yes. Designers, builders and contractors can apply to our Trade Program for preferred pricing, quotations and dedicated support.

What is your return policy?

Eligible unused items in original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Special-order and made-to-order items are final sale.
