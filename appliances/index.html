Frequently Asked Questions

Appliances FAQ

How do I choose the right range size?

Common widths are 30, 36, 40 and 48 inches. Measure the cabinet opening and consider how many burners and ovens you actually use.

Do I need a special range hood for a professional-style range?

Yes. Choose a hood at least as wide as the cooktop, with enough airflow (CFM) for its total burner output. Ask us for a recommendation.

How are appliances delivered?

Appliances ship by freight. We contact you to arrange a delivery appointment, and you should inspect the appliance before signing the delivery receipt.

Do appliances come with a warranty?

Yes, every appliance carries the manufacturer’s warranty. Warranty terms are listed on each product page.
