Information We Collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, phone, shipping and billing address, order details, trade account and project information, messages and uploaded files.
  • Payment information: processed securely by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Automatic information: device, browser, pages viewed and cookies, as described below.

How We Use It

To process and deliver orders, provide customer and trade support, respond to enquiries, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations and, with your consent, send marketing emails and improve our website.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the business (payment processing, shipping carriers, manufacturers fulfilling your order, email and hosting services), and where required by law. Some providers may store information outside your province or country.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use essential cookies without consent because the store can't work without them. We only use analytics and marketing cookies if you allow them. You can change your choice at any time via .

Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law, you may request access to or correction of your personal information, and withdraw consent to its use, subject to legal limits. Quebec residents have additional rights under Law 25, including data portability and information about automated decisions. To make a request, contact our privacy officer below.

US Residents

Depending on your state, you may have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and to opt out of its “sale” or “sharing” for targeted advertising. Use or contact us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Retention & Security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and our legal obligations, and protect it with appropriate safeguards.
