Our Story

Everything for a Beautiful Home

Bliss Bath and Kitchen is a Canadian destination for luxury bath, kitchen, appliances and home.

For more than three decades, our team has worked within the kitchen and bath industry, building relationships with leading manufacturers and helping homeowners and industry professionals source exceptional products for their spaces. Today, we bring that experience online with a thoughtfully curated collection of fixtures, vanities, appliances, lighting and furniture from distinguished brands around the world.

Our philosophy is simple: creating a beautiful home should be inspiring, not overwhelming. Whether you're choosing a single faucet or sourcing products for an entire home, we bring the products, brands and expertise together in one place.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you shop with confidence.

Do you offer free shipping across Canada and the USA?

Yes. Free standard shipping applies to eligible orders under 70 lbs anywhere in Canada and the United States. Larger items such as bathtubs, vanities and appliances ship by freight, and we'll confirm any charges before your order is processed. Shipping policy

Can I get design advice or help with product selection?

Absolutely. Our product specialists can help you compare options, coordinate finishes and choose products that work together, in the showroom, by phone or by email.

How long will my order take to arrive?

In-stock products typically ship within 2–5 business days, then arrive in about 2–5 business days by ground. Items not currently in stock usually need about 2 weeks before shipping, and special-order products take longer. We'll email you tracking or a lead time. Shipping & lead times

Do you offer trade or project pricing?

Yes. Designers, builders, contractors and other professionals can apply for a trade account with preferred pricing, or send us a product list for project and volume pricing. Trade program

What is your return policy?

Eligible products may be returned within 14 days of delivery if unused, uninstalled and in original packaging. All returns need prior authorization and are subject to a 25% restocking fee plus shipping. Full return policy

Do you have a showroom I can visit?

Yes, our showroom is at 5 Shields Court, Unit 104, Markham, Ontario. Drop in, or book an appointment for dedicated help with your project.

Do you install the products you sell?

We don't install, but we strongly recommend a licensed, qualified installer and can refer trusted professionals in your area. Please inspect every item before installation. Installing a damaged product can affect your return and warranty options.

Are your products covered by a warranty?

Yes. Products carry the manufacturer's warranty, with terms set by each brand (for example, many freestanding tubs include a 25-year limited warranty). We're happy to help you register a product or contact the manufacturer.
