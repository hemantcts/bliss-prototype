Free Shipping

We offer free standard shipping on eligible orders weighing less than 70 lbs within Canada and the United States. Some exclusions may apply depending on the product, delivery location, manufacturer requirements or shipping method. Oversized products, remote-area deliveries and items requiring freight service may be subject to additional charges. If additional shipping charges apply, our team will contact you before the order is processed.

Standard Ground Shipping

Most orders under 70 lbs ship by standard ground service. Once your order has shipped, tracking information will be provided by email whenever available. Most ground shipments arrive approximately 2–5 business days after leaving the warehouse. A signature is not normally required for small-parcel deliveries; final delivery procedures are determined by the carrier and driver.

Freight Delivery for Large & Oversized Items

Products weighing 70 lbs or more, and large or oversized products such as vanities, bathtubs, large sinks and certain appliances, may require freight delivery.

Standard freight is curbside or driveway delivery only. It does not include bringing merchandise inside your home, garage, business or job site. Someone must be available to receive and inspect the shipment. Carriers typically call to arrange a 2–4 hour delivery window during regular business hours.

If the carrier cannot contact you or complete a scheduled delivery, additional storage, redelivery or return freight charges may be your responsibility. For special delivery requirements, please contact our team before ordering. See inspecting freight deliveries.

Expedited Shipping

Next Day, 2-Day and 3-Day service may be available for select products. Expedited shipping only reduces the carrier's transit time after the product has shipped. It does not reduce manufacturer lead times, special-order production times or order processing time. Bliss Bath & Kitchen is not responsible for expedited shipping charges or other costs resulting from manufacturer, processing or lead-time delays. For expedited options, call Customer Service at 1-855-366-1001 before placing your order.

Local Markham Warehouse Pickup

Customers may pick up eligible orders from our warehouse in Markham, Ontario. Once your order is ready, we'll email pickup instructions. Please wait for this confirmation before arriving. You may also arrange for your own shipping company to collect merchandise with our prior approval.

Shipping & Delivery Times

In-stock products

Typically ship within 2–5 business days from the order date; most ground orders then arrive within 2–5 business days. “In Stock” may include inventory held by Bliss or reported as available by a manufacturer or distributor through a real-time feed. All inventory is subject to prior sale.

Products not currently in stock

Typically require approximately 2 weeks before shipment, though lead times vary by manufacturer and product. Standard delivery after shipping is generally 2–5 business days.

Special-order, custom & made-to-order products

May have longer lead times. Where available we'll email an estimated lead time. We strongly recommend ordering well ahead of your installation or project deadline.

Backorders & Inventory Changes

Availability can change quickly. If an item becomes backordered, we'll notify you and you may be able to:

  • Continue with the order and wait for the product
  • Select an alternative product
  • Cancel the backordered item for a refund

Approximate Shipping & Lead Times

All shipping, processing and delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed. Manufacturer delays, inventory changes, production schedules, carrier delays, weather, customs and other circumstances outside our control may affect delivery. Bliss Bath & Kitchen is not responsible for costs associated with delays, including contractor, installation or labour charges.

Please don't schedule installation until all products have been received, inspected and confirmed correct and undamaged. Working to a deadline? Call 1-855-366-1001 before ordering so we can confirm availability.

International Shipping

For destinations outside Canada and the United States, contact admin@blissbathandkitchen.com or 1-855-366-1001 for availability and a quote. International and cross-border shipments may be subject to customs clearance, tariffs, duties, brokerage fees, taxes and other import charges, which are not included in product or shipping prices unless stated and are the customer's responsibility. Carriers or customs authorities may request additional information to complete clearance.
