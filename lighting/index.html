Lighting turns a well-designed room into a beautiful one. Layer statement chandeliers and pendants with vanity lights and sconces, in warm brass, polished nickel and matte black finishes that coordinate with your fixtures.

Plan three layers of light: ambient for overall brightness, task light where you work, and accent light to highlight architecture and art.

Frequently Asked Questions

Lighting FAQ

What size chandelier do I need?

Add the room’s length and width in feet and use the total as the chandelier diameter in inches. Over a table, choose about half to two-thirds of the table width.

How high should pendants hang over an island?

Hang pendants roughly 30–36 inches above the countertop, spaced evenly along the island.

Are your light fixtures dimmable?

Most are dimmable with a compatible dimmer and bulbs. Check the specifications on each product page.

Do you ship lighting across Canada and the USA?

Yes. Eligible orders ship free across Canada and the USA.
